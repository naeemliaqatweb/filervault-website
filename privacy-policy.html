Privacy Policy & Data Sovereignty

FilerVault is built on a non-negotiable architectural commitment: 100% on-device data sovereignty. Your fiscal records, taxable income, and wealth declarations never transmit to our servers or any third-party cloud.

Privacy Guarantee

  • No Cloud Servers: Zero backend ingestion.
  • Hardware Keystore: AES-256 on-device key derivation.
  • Zero Analytics: No tracking SDKs or ad networks.
  • Local SMS Regex: SMS parsing never leaves phone memory.
  • FBR Sec 116 Compliant: Structured for Pakistani filers.

1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy governs your use of the software application FilerVault ("Application", "we", "us", or "our"), created by Naeem Liaqat.

FilerVault is an offline-first tax management, withholding tax (WHT) ledger, and wealth reconciliation companion engineered specifically for Pakistani tax filers under the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) legal framework (Income Tax Ordinance 2001).

Our guiding principle is Zero-Cloud Telemetry. We believe that personal wealth declarations and tax identifiers are sensitive confidential assets that should remain under the exclusive physical control of the device owner.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect (Zero Collection)

Unlike conventional cloud-based accounting apps, we do not operate any user database or cloud synchronization backend. Specifically:

  • No Account Registration: You do not need to sign up with an email, phone number, or password on any external server.
  • No Financial Telemetry: Your declared income, assets, properties, bank account details, and WHT deduction receipts are NEVER uploaded to any remote server.
  • No Tracking IDs: We do not track Advertising Identifiers (GAID/IDFA), IP addresses, or location coordinates.
  • No Third-Party Analytics SDKs: FilerVault contains zero Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDKs, Adjust, AppsFlyer, or third-party behavioral trackers.

3. On-Device Storage & Encryption Architecture

All data you input into FilerVault is stored exclusively in your device's local sandbox using SQLite and MMKV/Encrypted SharedPreferences:

  • Local Enclave: Database files reside exclusively in the protected application directory: /data/data/com.filervault/databases/.
  • Hardware Key Derivation: Cryptographic master keys are derived and locked via the Android Keystore system and optional Biometric Authentication (Fingerprint / Face Unlock).
  • BIP-39 Sovereign Mnemonic: The app generates a 12-word cryptographic seed phrase locally on your phone, ensuring you can restore your vault offline without any cloud dependency.

4. SMS Permission & On-Device Regex Parsing

FilerVault offers an optional utility to extract withholding tax (Section 236/231) deductions from Pakistani banking SMS alerts (e.g., Meezan Bank, HBL, UBL, JazzCash, Nayapay, SadaPay).

Strict SMS Privacy Guarantee:

1. SMS messages are processed solely in volatile device memory using deterministic regular expressions.
2. No message body or sender metadata is stored in plaintext or transmitted over the network.
3. You can disable SMS parsing anytime in Settings with a single switch.

5. User Control & Data Deletion

Because all data is stored on your local phone, you have 100% complete ownership and control:

  • Instant Wipe: You can purge all ledgers, keys, and cached records instantaneously via Settings > Danger Zone > Wipe All Vault Records.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling FilerVault immediately destroys all local databases and sandbox keys.
  • Encrypted Offline Export: You can export full AES-256 encrypted backups or CSV files directly to your device storage.

6. Children’s Privacy

FilerVault is designed for adult tax filers and financial record keepers. We do not knowingly collect, store, or process any personal data from individuals under the age of 18.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published on this URL (https://naeemliaqatweb.github.io/filervault/privacy-policy.html) and will maintain our foundational commitment to 100% offline data sovereignty.
